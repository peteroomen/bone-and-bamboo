Bone & Bamboo Hand Mock

A hand in progress at phone size, the same moment in each colourway. The tiles' bodies, the guide and the frames are drawn in code. Every tile face and the dragons' pictures are generated once, traced, and recoloured per colourway; the fox is a generated shadow-theatre painting. A mock of the layout, not the game.

  • East wind, round 1, hosted by the Fox Spirit. The target is already beaten; every 25% over it pays extra cash, so you keep building. Two sets are on the table and the run 6-7-8 of Characters is selected in the hand.
  • The numbers are real: table now 119 chips × 13 mult = 1,547 (Coin Purse +36 chips for three Dots tiles, Abacus +2 per run, Red String +6). Playing the run makes it 150 × 16 = 2,400.
  • The wall shows each stack's top tile and the top strip of the one beneath (the corner index), with the count left in the stack.
