# Dice: starting cup, pattern values and targets (16 Oct 2026)

Simulator: `pnpm sim` (`src/sim/`). Rounds are played by the guide's advice (`src/engine/advice.ts`):
it picks the plan that scores most per roll, keeping a quarter of the rolls in hand, takes any set
that fits, uses charms that make a set, and otherwise holds the dice that give the best chance of a
set it needs next roll. It sees only public information (dice faces). Shoppers: `smart` makes the
cup one suit and buys scoring and dice charms; `casual` buys at random. Both bank as soon as the
target is beaten.

## Starting cup

Rounds of 20-24 rolls with no charms, 80-100 rounds each, guide's pick of plan.

| Cup | Rolls to the first hand | Hands a round | Note |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 3 bamboo + 2 dots cubes (5) | 15.5 | 0.5 | two dots dice only ever make pairs |
| 5 bamboo cubes | 6.7 | 3.0 (20 rolls) | every hand a Full Flush |
| 3 bamboo + 2 half-and-half (5) | 8.3 | 2.3 (20 rolls) | Full Flush nearly free |
| 5 two-suit cubes | 13.5-16 | 0.9 | too slow |
| 5 long dice (4 faces) | 13-16 | 0.4-1.0 | narrow: few sets at all |
| 6 two-suit cubes, staggered (**chosen**) | 10.4 | 2.0 (24 rolls) | |
| 6 one-suit cubes, 3 + 3 | 11.9 | 1.6 (24 rolls) | Seven Pairs dominates |

What decides speed is how many dice can take part in a set: a chow needs three dice of one suit
with neighbouring faces. Concentrating a suit is fast *and* flushes, so the starting cup splits two
suits across every die.

## What each pattern costs (starting cup, target set, 24 rolls, 60 rounds)

| Pattern | Rolls to finish | Fits |
|---|---:|---:|
| Chicken | 9.3 | 100% |
| All Simples | 9.5 | 100% |
| All Chows | 10.1 | 100% |
| Seven Pairs | 10.6 | 100% |
| Full Flush | 11.4 | 98% |
| All Pongs | 18.9 | 85% |
| everything else | not reachable with the starting cup | |

Full Flush costs only 1.2 Chicken hands with dice, so it is ×2 (it is the dearest suit pattern in
the table game). All Pongs at ×3 with pong sets at 30 chips / +3 mult paid ten times a Chicken hand
and the bot threw away 10% of first rounds chasing it: pongs are now 25 / +2 and All Pongs ×2.5.
Honour hands need three dice sharing each honour: they are the late, built hands.

The guide's roll estimate (shown in the hand book) is 2 + 2.4 × the sum over the open slots of
1 / (the summed rates of the sets that fit the slot), where a set's rate is 1 / its mean rolls with
holding. Fitted to the table above: Chicken 8.8, All Chows 12.2, Full Flush 15.6, All Pongs 16.4.

effect on its choices beyond playing them. Humans will differ: playtest first, then tune.

## Known limits

- The smart shopper is narrow (one suit, scoring charms). Honour dice, tools other than Brush,
  Chisel, Lead and the leaves, and pattern pages for big hands are barely tested.
- The round-1 median hand (about 12,000) is far above its target: the first hand often fits
  All Simples and Full Flush. Early rounds are easy on purpose for now.
- Charms that bend dice (Thumb, Mirror, Shell) are used only when they make a set at once.
