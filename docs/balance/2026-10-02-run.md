# Bone & Bamboo: run simulator results (2 October 2026)

`runsim.py` plays whole runs: 4 rounds, 3 teahouses, $4 to start, paid $10 / $12 / $14 after
rounds 1-3 (+ $1 per unused discard, + $1 interest per $5 held, max $5). Round rules: 81 tiles
(1-9, 3 copies), hand 8, 8 stacks, see 1 under each top, 8 plays, 3 discards; pair 5/+1,
chow 10/+1, pong 40/+4, kong 100/+8. 21 curios (8 common $4, 7 uncommon $6, 6 rare $8),
almanac pages $3, fortunes $3 (copy, fire, brush, jade, bone), tile packs $4-5.

Two shoppers. **Smart** tries every affordable item on 12 seeded rounds and buys the best gain per
mon; it also picks how to play (chows or pongs) for its build. **Casual** buys at random. Bots
show what the rules allow; they are not forecasts of human win rates.

## Recommended

- Targets **1,000 / 4,000 / 9,000 / 18,000** for the first difficulty.
- **Each calmed spirit leaves a gift**: pick 1 of 2 rare curios, free.
- **Fire burns 6 tiles** (was 2).
- Result: smart wins 71%, casual 24%. Nobody loses in round 1. Lanterns (difficulty) raise the targets from here.

## Findings

1. **A round-1 target above about 1,000 kills runs before any choice is made** (17% at 1,500). East must be gentle.
2. **Rare builds need the spirit's gift.** Three shops show only nine curio offers in a run. Without gifts, Kong Bell and Dragon Lantern almost never appear. With gifts, rare curios reach 15-50% of finished builds.
3. **Reshaping your set needs a strong Fire.** All Simples needs 24 tiles removed. At 2 per Fire its builds won 63%; at 6, 81%.
4. **Almanac pages are the most bought item** (about 1.3 chow and 1.3 pong pages a run). $3 may be too cheap. Watch them.
5. **The rare curios mostly reward chows** (Pure Straight, Twin Cranes, All Simples). With gifts, the smart bot plays chows in 83% of finished builds, down from 60%. Add rare pong and kong curios to keep both strategies alive.
6. **Weak:** Iron Teapot (+2 discards, 53% win when owned), Pong Hall (needs 2+ pongs, 55-67%). **Strong:** Night Owl (+1 play, 82-93%), Sparrow's Nest (pairs +6 mult, 92%).
7. Not modelled yet: flowers in the wall, spirit twists, rerolls, selling, enhancements beyond jade and bone, lanterns.
