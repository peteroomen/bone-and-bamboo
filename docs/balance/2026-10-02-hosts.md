# Bone & Bamboo: the eight twists (M5, 2 October 2026)

`pnpm sim hosts --runs 400` plays 400 smart-shopper runs on the recommended ladder (1,000 / 4,000 /
9,000 / 18,000, gift, fire 6) five times with the same seeds: once with every round calm, and once
for each wind with only that wind's round stormy. The builds are identical up to the wind in
question, so each row compares the calm and storm twist of a wind on the same players. The storm's
target is ×1.5 (`STORM_TARGET_MULT`); its bigger gift (3 dragons and $5) is in the sim.

| Wind | Twist | Round win rate | Median score ÷ target | Runs reaching it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| East | Masked (calm) | 100% | 7.67 | 400 |
| East | The coil (storm) | 99% | 2.61 | 400 |
| South | Swaps (calm) | 90% | 2.60 | 400 |
| South | Embers (storm) | 83% | 1.95 | 400 |
| West | Moon tide (calm) | 95% | 3.43 | 360 |
| West | Claws (storm) | **73%** | 1.88 | 360 |
| North | The report (calm) | 81% | 2.44 | 341 |
| North | The shell (storm) | **53%** | 1.06 | 341 |

**Flagged (storm more than 15 points under the calm twist of its wind): Claws (−22) and The shell
(−28).** Masked is not flagged on win rate but is far out on score (median 7.7× the target, about
three times any other round), see below.

## What the numbers say

- **Masked is too strong as written.** "+2 mult for each tile you play that you took while it was
  hidden" fires for nearly every tile (all but the 8 stack tops dealt), so a table of 20 tiles gets
  about +40 mult. I implemented it that way (the design's words) and recommend a smaller number or
  "+2 mult per set that contains one". The hiding itself (no strips: the tile under a top is not
  shown) barely costs the bot, which weighs the strips lightly.
- **Claws** halves chows, which are 5.5 of the bot's 8 plays, and doubles pongs, which it plays
  1.2 times. The bot (greedy) does not change its play; a human would hunt pongs. Both facts point
  at the bot, but the ×1.5 target on top is steep for a chow build.
- **The shell** is the hardest wind. Its armour has to be read somehow (see Questions): I made the
  armoured tiles the 6 stack tops dealt, which cannot be discarded until a pong or kong is played.
  North is also the last round with the highest target, so ×1.5 of 18,000 is 27,000.
- **The report** (calm North) is about as hard as the untwisted round: −25 a discard is small next
  to 18,000, and the bot uses its discards, so it rarely gets the ×2.
- **Moon tide** is easy: one more tile in hand, and the discards are not lost.

## What the bot does not do

It never uses the monkey's own swap, never avoids or hunts burning tiles, and does not plan around
hidden strips or the shell's armour. A human who does will do better under Swaps and Embers.
Win rates are bot results, not human forecasts.

## After the planner's tuning (applied)

`pnpm sim hosts --runs 400`, smart shopper, same seeds. Changes: Masked per set (+2, then +1);
Claws without the chow half; the shell lifted by any set, with `targetMult: 1.25`.

| Wind | Host | Round win rate | Median score ÷ target | Runs reaching it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| East | Masked (calm) | 100% | 3.17 | 400 |
| East | The coil (storm, ×1.5) | 99% | 2.61 | 400 |
| South | Swaps (calm) | 90% | 2.60 | 400 |
| South | Embers (storm, ×1.5) | 83% | 1.95 | 400 |
| West | Moon tide (calm) | 95% | 3.43 | 360 |
| West | Claws (storm, ×1.5) | 82% | 2.34 | 360 |
| North | The report (calm) | 81% | 2.44 | 341 |
| North | The shell (storm, ×1.25) | 64% | 1.29 | 341 |

- Masked at +2 per set was still 4.66× the target (over the 3.5× limit), so it is **+1 per set**: 3.17×.
- Claws is 82%, level with the other storms.
- The shell with any-set armour was 54% at ×1.5; at the fallback ×1.25 it is 64%, still 17 points under
  The report. The armour is the hard part; left for the planner to judge in play.
