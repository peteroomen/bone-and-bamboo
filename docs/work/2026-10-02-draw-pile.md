# The draw pile replaces the wall

## Goal

Playtest feedback: the wall of stacks is confusing. Replace it with a Balatro-style draw pile: your
set is shuffled face down, your hand refills on its own after every play and discard, and a round
is just _plays_ and _discards_.

## Approach

- The engine keeps one hidden stack (the pile). After the deal, each play, upgrade and discard,
  the hand refills from the top of the pile. There is no take action any more.
- Drawing blind loses the choice the wall gave: `pnpm sim round` (greedy, 2,000 rounds) drops
  from a median 3,296 to 1,911 at hand 8 / discards 3, with 2.6 dead single plays a round.
  Hand and discards were swept to win the choice back:

  | Hand        | Discards | p25   | Median | p75   | Singles a round |
  | ----------- | -------- | ----- | ------ | ----- | --------------- |
  | wall (8, 3) |          | 2,595 | 3,296  | 4,199 | 0.3             |
  | 8           | 3        | 1,548 | 1,911  | 2,432 | 2.6             |
  | 9           | 3        | 1,834 | 2,268  | 2,848 | 1.7             |
  | 10          | 3        | 2,176 | 2,656  | 3,420 | 0.9             |
  | 10          | 4        | 2,280 | 2,832  | 3,724 | 0.7             |
  | 11          | 4        | 2,608 | 3,196  | 4,194 | 0.3             |
  | **12**      | **4**    | 2,880 | 3,591  | 4,484 | 0.1             |

  Hand 11 and 4 discards matches the wall's round scores, but whole runs lost more (the dragon
  builds leaned on picking tiles). **Chosen: hand 12, 4 discards, targets 1,000 / 3,600 / 8,000 /
  16,000**, which gives the wall's win rates (`docs/balance/2026-10-02-draw-pile.md`).

- Content that needed the wall:
  - Lantern: "see the next 3 tiles of the pile" (the pile's top tiles shown face up).
  - Masked (East, calm): your hand is 1 smaller; each set of 3 or more tiles gives +1 mult.
  - The coil (East, storm): no discarding until you play a chow; chows score double chips.
  - Swaps (South, calm): after every 2nd play, one random tile in your hand goes back into the
    pile and you draw another; once a round you may swap a tile of your choice the same way.
  - Embers (South, storm): 4 tiles in your set are burning; a burning tile in a played set gives
    +4 mult; one held in your hand for 2 turns burns away.
  - Moon tide (West, calm): hand +1; discards are shuffled back into the pile.
  - The shell (North, storm): your first hand is armoured (can't be discarded) until you play a
    set; kongs +4 mult.
  - Claws and The report are unchanged.
- The UI loses the wall: the hand gets the space, with bigger tiles; the pile shows as a count
  (and Lantern's next tiles). Auto and draw advice go; Ask advises plays and discards. Jade Court
  becomes hand 13 / 3 discards and Lantern 4 3 discards (each one off the new base, as before).

## Steps

1. Engine: auto-draw, remove `take` and the run's `auto`; twists reworked; bot and advice.
2. Sim: round, run and hosts tables; calibrate the reworked twists.
3. UI: no wall, pile counter, larger hand, guide tips and help text.
4. Docs: design.md, playtest notes; tests and e2e.

## Manual test

Start a run: the hand arrives full (12 tiles; 11 under Masked). Play a set: the hand refills at once. Discard:
new tiles arrive. Try each wind's twist.

## Out of scope

Art (M9). Retuning targets beyond what the sim shows is needed.
