# Bone dice: mahjong with dice

## Goal

Replace the card-style round (play sets from a hand) with dice: every face a tile, Yahtzee-style
holds, sets taken onto a rack, four sets and a pair a hand, a shared roll budget per round. Keep
the tiles, sets, scoring count, tile art and sound. Make it look like a game: a 3D table.

Decisions from the user (16 Oct): take whole sets from the dice (not single tiles); one roll budget
per round shared across hands; build on a new branch; the Thumb is a charm, Lead Weight is a dice
edit.

## Approach

- **Engine** (`src/engine`, pure): dice and shapes (`dice.ts`), sets and the rack (`sets.ts`),
  patterns as slot variants (`patterns.ts`), scoring (`scoring.ts`), the round reducer
  (`round.ts`), tools (`tools.ts`), the run with gifts and teahouse (`run.ts`), and the planner
  (`advice.ts`): exact chances for the next roll, plans per pattern, hold advice. The planner is the
  hint system, the hand book's estimates and the simulator's player.
- **Content** (`src/content`): shapes, dice templates and the starting cup, patterns, charms,
  tools, hosts and twists, rules and targets.
- **Simulator** (`src/sim`): `pnpm sim` plays whole runs; results in
  `docs/balance/2026-10-16-dice.md`.
- **UI** (`src/ui`): React Three Fiber table (`src/ui/three`): tray, dice, rack, physics throws
  with a landing correction; HTML HUD over it (`src/ui/game`). Old card UI removed on this branch.

## Steps (done)

1. Engine and content, unit tests (`src/engine/engine.test.ts`).
2. Simulator; starting cup chosen; pattern values from measured roll costs; targets.
3. 3D table: dice models with per-face textures and symmetry groups (tested), cannon-es throws,
   rack tiles flying in from the dice.
4. HUD: score plaque, charms (tap to use Thumb, Shell, Fan, Mirror), take buttons, roll, bank,
   the guide's line, hand book with targets and ghost tiles, die sheet, return sheet, score count,
   cash-out, gifts, teahouse with dice editing, end screen, title, settings, first-run help.
5. Playwright at 390×844, 360×640 and 1366×768 (`e2e/game.spec.ts`), screenshots in
   `test-results/`.

## Manual test steps

1. `pnpm dev`, open on a phone. New run: the how-to-play sheet opens once.
2. Roll. Dice tumble and land; the guide's line gives a chance for the next roll.
3. Tap a die: a gold ring, and it stays put on the next roll. Hold a die down: its faces.
4. Take a set from the buttons: its tiles fly to the rack. Tap a rack set: send it back.
5. Hands: pick a pattern. Ghost tiles on the rack show what is missing.
6. Finish a hand: the Mahjong! count. Beat the target: Bank. Cash out, then the teahouse:
   buy a die (with a full cup, choose one to give up), a tool (use it on a face), a charm.
7. Round 2 is East's: Gusts lets you hold two dice. Then East's gift.
8. With a Thumb: tap the charm, tap a die, pick the face to tip onto.
9. Settings: speed Instant skips every animation; colourways change the dice and table.
