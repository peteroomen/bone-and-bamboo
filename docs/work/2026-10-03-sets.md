# Any number of sets a play, and sorting the hand

## Goal

Playtest (3 Oct, on the pile): "I was surprised not to be able to play 4 in a row, and the
ability to play more than one set at once. I never tried rearranging either."

## Update (3 Oct, later)

The user asked to keep to the **standard sets** for now: the runs of four and five are taken out
again (they are still in the sim table below, as tried). Without them five plays came out a little
hard (pile 61% / 31%, wall 64% / 28%), so the targets came down about 6%: pile 1,000 / 3,400 /
7,500 / 15,000 (65% / 37%), wall 1,000 / 3,600 / 8,000 / 16,000 (67% / 32%). Rearranging tiles
already on the table, Rummikub style, is noted for later.

## Update (3 Oct, later still)

"Just n sets per play: if you had 3 pairs that's valid." The two-set cap is lifted (`setsPerPlay`
99). With no cap and 5 plays the runs were easy (pile 76% / 43%, wall 75% / 40%; 4 plays was
47% / 18%), so plays stay at 5 and targets go up: pile back to 1,000 / 4,000 / 9,000 / 18,000
(69% / 34%), wall 1,000 / 4,200 / 9,500 / 19,000 (67% / 30%). Per wind:
`docs/balance/2026-10-03-sets.md`.

## Approach (as first built)

- **Runs of four and five:** new sets _Four in a row_ (25 chips, +2 mult) and _Five in a row_
  (50, +3), between a chow and a pong. They count as runs for Abacus (now "+2 mult per run") and
  the coil (now "no discarding until you play a run; runs score double chips"); Pure Straight
  stays chow-only. No almanac page levels them yet.
- **Several sets in one play:** pick the tiles of up to two sets and tap Play; the engine splits
  the selection every way it can (`partitions` in `src/engine/sets.ts`) and keeps the split that
  scores the table highest. A single is never part of a bigger play.
- **Plays:** two sets a play nearly doubles scores at 8 plays, so plays drop to **5**, as in
  Balatro's few hands. That keeps each play a choice: table two sets now, or hold a pair for a
  pong. Targets stay.
- **Sort:** the Clear slot shows Sort when nothing is selected: the hand by suit, or by number.
  Remembered in settings.

## Manual test

Pick a pong and a chow together: the preview adds both; Play puts both on the table for one
play. Pick 4 in a row: it plays as Four in a row. Tap Sort: the hand regroups by number; again,
by suit.

## Out of scope

Dragging tiles to reorder the hand; rearranging tiles already on the table (Rummikub style).
